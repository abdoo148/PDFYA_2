Before installing PDFYA
=======================

1) "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)
   This appears because the app is new and not code-signed yet — not because something is wrong.
   To continue: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

2) Antivirus
   Some antivirus programs may flag the app by mistake because it is built with Python and isn't signed.
   The app is fully open source: https://github.com/abdoo148/PDFYA_2
   If the file is blocked, add it as an exception, and only download it from the official link.

3) No administrator rights are needed; the app is installed for your account only.
   You can choose to install it for all users on the first screen if you prefer.

4) The first launch may take a few seconds (the PDFYA screen appears while it loads).

5) Optional features that need extra free programs:
   - Word / Excel / PowerPoint to PDF: needs Microsoft Office or LibreOffice.
   - Text recognition (OCR): needs Tesseract — select Arabic during its installation:
     https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki
   Everything else works right away with no extras.

6) Privacy: the app runs entirely on your computer and never uploads your files.
   Your signature library and settings are stored in %APPDATA%\PDFYA.

7) Signatures in PDFYA are images placed on the page, not certificate-based digital signatures.

8) To uninstall: Settings > Apps > PDFYA > Uninstall.
